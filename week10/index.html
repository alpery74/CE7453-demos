Each layer is X_{k+1} = D^{-1/2} (A + I) D^{-1/2} X_k with the weight matrix set to the identity, which is the Kipf and Welling rule on slide 7 with the learnable part removed. The pulse is the signal; the animation is the sum over neighbours that every junction computes at once.

Break the model: renumber the scan and see which model notices

Eight points from a laser scan, listed in file order. Two models read the file. Your job is to change each model's answer. One of them will not let you.

the file, in order (drag rows or use the arrows)

    challenges

    1. Change the red model's output by at least 0.5 without moving any point. not yet
    2. Reorder the file to change the blue model's output by 0.05. Can you? not yet

    The red model multiplies the 16 concatenated coordinates by a fixed matrix, so it reads the order you typed the points in. The blue model applies one shared encoder to every point and takes the maximum over points. Order is bookkeeping and geometry is data; a model for scans and graphs must answer to the second and ignore the first, which is the constraint on slides 6 and 10.

    Design the operating policy, then see how close you got

Then click cells to set the direction the crew takes from each one, evaluate your policy exactly and compare it with the optimum.     Evaluating a fixed policy solves V = r + gamma P V, one linear system. The reveal runs value iteration, V(s) = max over actions of expected reward plus gamma V(next), one sweep at a time, and the right panel shows the largest change per sweep shrinking at rate gamma. Reading the policy off the values is the last line of slide 16.

    The exploration race

    Two crews learn the same site from experience with Q-learning. The left crew always takes its current best guess. The right crew acts at random a fraction of the time, set by the sector on the coin between the two grids: drag its edge. Place your bet by clicking a crew's grid, or the tie flag under the coin, then start the race. After the race a green dot walks each crew's final greedy path.
