The picture at step t is x_t = sqrt(abar_t) x_0 + sqrt(1 - abar_t) eps, the closed form on slide 4, with one fixed noise draw per round. The schedule beta_t rises linearly from 0.0001 to 0.02 over T = 1000 steps, so abar_t falls below one half near t = 260 and below 0.02 by t = 600. Past that point every step looks the same, which is why the eye judges signal-to-noise, not t.

    The sampler is DDIM with the exact posterior-mean denoiser of a Gaussian prior over smooth patches (a 10 by 10 cosine basis), so the only approximation is the step placement.     Every sample follows eps_hat = eps_u + w (eps_c - eps_u), the line on slide 9, through 1000 ancestral steps, with exact noise predictions for the two Gaussian mixtures so that nothing is trained. w = 0 ignores the request, w = 1 is plain conditional sampling, and w above one sharpens the class at the price of diversity. Both predictions are needed per step, so guidance doubles the cost of every sample.

    Each fit minimizes L = L_data + lambda L_pde + w_bc L_bc, slide 14, over 40 radial basis functions with w_bc fixed and the PDE residual normalized by the size of the source. Small lambda ignores the equation and interpolates three noisy points; large lambda ignores the gauges and solves an equation whose source is wrong. The valley between them moves as the source error grows, which is the stochastic model decision on slide 16.
