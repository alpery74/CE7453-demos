Nothing here is learned. The schedule beta_t rises linearly from 0.0001 to 0.02, so abar_t, the product of (1 - beta), decays as the right panel shows. By about t = 300 the field is hard to see; by t = 1000 the signal fraction is below 0.0001 and what remains is noise.

The same noise draw eps is used for every t, which is why the picture changes continuously as you scrub. Training picks a random t and jumps straight to that frame.

To keep this honest the prior over fields is a Gaussian process with a known kernel, so the ideal denoiser E[x_0 | x_t] has a closed form and no network is needed. Every difference between the blue and grey curves is the sampler's own error from taking too few steps.

With one step the sampler returns the posterior mean of pure noise, which is the prior mean. With a handful of steps the field is too smooth and its spread is far below the prior's. Past a few dozen steps the sample stops changing, which is the plateau on slide 8.

The combined prediction is eps_hat = eps(x, empty) + w (eps(x, c) - eps(x, empty)), exactly the line on slide 9, run through 1000 ancestral reverse steps with the exact scores of this toy model. At w = 0 the condition is ignored. At w = 1 the histogram matches the true conditional.

Push w past 1 and the draws cluster more tightly than p(x | c) allows: the guided sampler is biased toward a sharpened distribution, and the spread readout drops below the true value of 0.35. That is the variety you give up for agreement with the condition.

The loss is L = L_data + lambda L_pde + w_bc L_bc, as on slide 14, with w_bc fixed. The function class here is a set of 40 radial basis functions, so the loss is quadratic in the coefficients and the fit is one linear solve per slider move; a network would give the same picture more slowly.

At the left end of the slider the equation is ignored: the three gauges are hit and everything between them is invented. At the right end the data is ignored: the model obeys the equation it was given, and if that equation carries a wrong source term the field is confidently wrong. The right panel sweeps the weight and marks where you are.
